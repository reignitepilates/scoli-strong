Learn the basics

A practical scoliosis guide

Scoliosis is a three-dimensional curvature of the spine. Evaluation and management depend on age, curve size, symptoms, and other individual factors. This is general education, not a diagnosis.

01 / Understanding

What is scoliosis?

Learn what a spinal curve is, how it is measured, and why different types of scoliosis require different approaches.

02 / Getting answers

Diagnosis & monitoring

Healthcare professionals may use an examination and imaging to assess spinal curvature and recommend follow-up.

03 / Finding support

Treatment conversations

Depending on the person, options may include monitoring, bracing, scoliosis-specific exercise, symptom management, or surgery.

For medical guidance, consult an appropriately qualified clinician. Read the AAOS scoliosis overview ↗
